# Christina Hoon — Bielefeld University, Germany

Qualitative methodologist best known for the meta-synthesis of qualitative case
studies (her ORM 2013 paper, h-index-leading work with FWCI ~77), plus work on
how case studies and review studies make theoretical contributions. Substantive
home is family business, HRM in family firms, and strategy process/practice.

**Representative method papers:**
- Hoon (2013), "Meta-Synthesis of Qualitative Case Studies: An Approach to Theory Building," *Organizational Research Methods* — eight-step design for synthesizing primary qualitative case studies into theory.
- Ridder, Hoon & McCandless Baluch (2012), "Entering a Dialogue: Positioning Case Study Findings towards Theory," *British Journal of Management* — how case studies position claims against extant theory.
- Hoon & Baluch (2019), "The Role of Dialectical Interrogation in Review Studies: Theorizing from What We See Rather Than What We Have Already Seen," *Journal of Management Studies* — theorizing in review-centric work.
- Ridder, Hoon & McCandless (2009), "The theoretical contribution of case study research to the field of strategy and management," *Research Methodology in Strategy and Management* (book chapter).
