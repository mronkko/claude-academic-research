# Andrea Whittle — Newcastle University Business School, UK

Andrea Whittle is a qualitative organization-studies scholar whose program centers on language-in-use: ethnomethodology, membership categorization analysis, discursive psychology, and critical discourse analysis. Applications include strategy-as-practice, sensemaking, power and politics, accountability (audit, banking crisis), identity, and impression management. They work with frequent co-author Frank Mueller. Recent work is increasingly reflexive about qualitative method itself: the epistemology of interview data, researcher impression management, and corpus linguistics as a bridge between quantitative and qualitative analysis. Career path: PhD Brunel (2003), Cardiff University (2005–2012), then Newcastle University (2009–present, with a University of Newcastle Australia affiliation also recorded). The most-cited work is Whittle & Spicer (2008), "Is Actor Network Theory Critique?" (*Organization Studies*, 370+ citations).

**Representative method papers:**
- Whittle & Reissner (2024), "Making Knowledge Claims from Qualitative Interviews: A Typology of Epistemological Modes," *British Journal of Management*: a typology of the kinds of knowledge claims interview data can support.
- Reissner & Whittle (2021), "Interview-based research in management and organisation studies: making sense of the plurality of methodological practices and presentational styles," *Qualitative Research in Organizations and Management*: maps the varieties of interview-based research and how it is presented.
- Mautner, Whittle & Gillings (2026), "Corpus linguistics for leadership studies: Bridging the quantitative-qualitative divide," *The Leadership Quarterly*: introduces corpus-linguistic methods to leadership research.
- Mueller & Whittle (2026), "Researcher impression management: mapping the dramaturgical practices of qualitative researchers," *Qualitative Research in Organizations and Management*: a dramaturgical account of how qualitative researchers manage impressions in research practice.
- Whittle & Mueller (2022), "Membership Categorisation Analysis," Cambridge University Press chapter: a method primer on MCA for organizational research (see also Whittle 2023, Elgar chapter on using MCA to study identities in talk and text).
- Whittle & Wilson (2015), "Ethnomethodology and the production of history: studying 'history-in-action'," *Business History*: an ethnomethodological approach to historical organizational analysis.
- Mueller & Whittle (2011), "Translating Management Ideas: A Discursive Devices Analysis," *Organization Studies*: develops a discursive-devices analytic approach.

**Would referee well:** discourse analysis (critical, discursive-psychological, ethnomethodological), conversation and membership-categorization analysis, qualitative interview epistemology, naturally-occurring talk and text data, dramaturgical and impression-management analyses, corpus-linguistic text analysis, language and sensemaking, strategy-as-practice and power and politics in organizations.
