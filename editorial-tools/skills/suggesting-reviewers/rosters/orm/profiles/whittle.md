# Andrea Whittle — Newcastle University Business School, UK

Senior qualitative and language-focused organization scholar, expert in
discourse analysis, discursive psychology, ethnomethodology and membership
categorization analysis. Recent work turns explicitly methodological: the
epistemology of interview-based research, researcher impression management,
and corpus linguistics as a bridge between quantitative and qualitative text
analysis.

**Representative method papers:**
- Whittle & Reissner (2024), "Making Knowledge Claims from Qualitative Interviews: A Typology of Epistemological Modes," *British Journal of Management*.
- Mautner, Whittle & Gillings (2026), "Corpus linguistics for leadership studies: Bridging the quantitative-qualitative divide," *The Leadership Quarterly*.
- Mueller & Whittle (2026), "Researcher impression management: mapping the dramaturgical practices of qualitative researchers," *Qualitative Research in Organizations and Management*.
- Mueller & Whittle (2011), "Translating Management Ideas: A Discursive Devices Analysis," *Organization Studies* — puts forward a discursive-devices analytic approach.
