# Anna Brown — University of Kent, UK

Quantitative psychometrician; the leading authority on Thurstonian IRT modeling of multidimensional forced-choice questionnaires and ipsative data, plus measurement invariance and response-bias/faking. Reviews IRT, measurement, and scale-development submissions with very high authority.

**Representative method papers:**
- Brown & Maydeu-Olivares (2011), "Item Response Modeling of Forced-Choice Questionnaires," *Educational and Psychological Measurement* — Thurstonian IRT model.
- Brown & Maydeu-Olivares (2012), "How IRT can solve problems of ipsative data in forced-choice questionnaires," *Psychological Methods* — ipsative-data solution.
- Guenole & Brown (2014), "The consequences of ignoring measurement invariance for path coefficients in structural equation models," *Frontiers in Psychology* — measurement invariance.
- Li, Zhang, Li, Sun & Brown (2024), "Mixed-Keying or Desirability-Matching in the Construction of Forced-Choice Measures?," *Organizational Research Methods* — forced-choice measure design.
