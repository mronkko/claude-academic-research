# Joseph S. Harrison — University of Tennessee Knoxville, USA

Joseph Harrison is a strategic-management scholar working on upper echelons and corporate governance — CEO personality, CEO turnover and dismissal, boards, and executive mobility. His methodological contribution centres on building measures and datasets from archival and textual sources: a validated linguistic tool for measuring CEO personality from unscripted speech, supervised machine-learning procedures to scale human-coded data, and, more recently, large-language-model approaches to executive personality assessment. Publishes mainly in *SMJ*, *AMJ*, *Journal of Management*, *Organization Science*, and *Leadership Quarterly*.

**Representative method papers:**
- Harrison, Thurgood, Boivie & Pfarrer (2019), "Measuring CEO personality: Developing, validating, and testing a linguistic tool," *Strategic Management Journal* — machine-learning text measure of Big Five CEO traits with validation.
- Harrison, Josefy, Kalm & Krause (2022), "Using supervised machine learning to scale human-coded data: A method and dataset in the board leadership context," *Strategic Management Journal* — method for extending hand-coded archival variables via supervised ML.
- Hyde, Bachura & Harrison (2022), "Garbage in, Garbage out: A Theory-Driven Approach to Improve Data Handling in Supervised Machine Learning," *Research Methodology in Strategy and Management* — data-handling guidance for ML-based measurement.
- Harrison, Boivie, Hubbard & Petrenko (2024), "Executive Personality Assessment With Large Language Models: Updating an Existing Tool and Advancing Similar Measures in Strategy and Management Research," *Research Methodology in Strategy and Management* — LLM-based unobtrusive measurement.
- Harrison & Boivie (2026), "Reaffirming the validity of unobtrusive personality measures: A comment on Harms et al. (2025) and guidance for the field," *Journal of Occupational and Organizational Psychology* — validity debate over at-a-distance personality measures.
- Gentry, Harrison, Quigley & Boivie (2021), "A database of CEO turnover and dismissal in S&P 1500 firms, 2000–2018," *Strategic Management Journal* — open archival dataset with coding protocol.

**Would referee well:** text-based and unobtrusive measurement of executive traits; validation of ML/NLP and LLM-derived constructs; scaling hand-coded archival data; dataset construction in strategy research; upper-echelons and governance studies using panel/archival data.
