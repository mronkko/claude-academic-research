# Joseph S. Harrison — University of Tennessee Knoxville, USA

Mid-career strategy and governance scholar (h-index 13; 2-year mean citedness
~9.7) whose methods contribution is unobtrusive measurement: building and
validating language-based (ML, and now LLM) measures of CEO personality, and
publishing open archival datasets for upper-echelons research.

**Representative method papers:**
- Harrison, Thurgood, Boivie & Pfarrer (2019), "Measuring CEO personality: Developing, validating, and testing a linguistic tool," *Strategic Management Journal*: develops and validates a text-based Big Five measure for CEOs.
- Gentry, Harrison, Quigley & Boivie (2021), "A database of CEO turnover and dismissal in S&P 1500 firms, 2000–2018," *Strategic Management Journal*: open-source dataset with comparison to three existing datasets and how the differences change findings.
- Harrison, Boivie, Hubbard & Petrenko (2024), "Executive Personality Assessment With Large Language Models: Updating an Existing Tool and Advancing Similar Measures in Strategy and Management Research," *Research Methodology in Strategy and Management*: an LLM redevelopment of the OLCPT.
- Harrison & Boivie (2026), "Reaffirming the validity of unobtrusive personality measures: A comment on Harms et al. (2025) and guidance for the field," *Journal of Occupational and Organizational Psychology*.

**Would referee well:** unobtrusive and text-based measurement of individual
differences, ML/LLM-based construct measurement and validation, archival
data construction and replication across datasets, upper-echelons and
governance panel designs, CEO succession and dismissal data.
