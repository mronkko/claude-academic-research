# José M. Cortina — The Ohio State University, USA

Past editor of ORM and an eminent quantitative methodologist; author of the canonical paper on coefficient alpha and a long line of work on interaction effects, moderated SEM, meta-analysis, effect sizes, and scale construction.

**Representative method papers:**

- Cortina, Chen & Dunlap (2001), "Testing Interaction Effects in LISREL," ORM — moderated SEM.
- Cortina (1993), "Interaction, Nonlinearity, and Multicollinearity: Implications for Multiple Regression," Journal of Management — moderated regression.
- Alley, Kadolkar, Gupta, Cortina & Winsler (2024), "Grammatical Redundancy in Scales: Using the 'ConGRe' Process to Create Better Measures," Journal of Management — scale construction.
