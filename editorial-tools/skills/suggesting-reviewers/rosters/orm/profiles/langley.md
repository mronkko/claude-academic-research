# Ann Langley — HEC Montréal, Canada

Foundational senior qualitative methodologist best known for process research methodology, strategies for theorizing from process data, and comparative qualitative theory-building approaches.

**Representative method papers:**
- Langley (1999), "Strategies for Theorizing from Process Data," *Academy of Management Review* — canonical typology of process data analysis strategies.
- Langley et al. (2013), "Process Studies of Change in Organization and Management: Unveiling Temporality, Activity, and Flow," *Academy of Management Journal* — process research methodology.
- Gehman, Glaser, Eisenhardt, Gioia, Langley & Corley (2017/2018), "Finding Theory–Method Fit: A Comparison of Three Qualitative Approaches to Theory Building," *Journal of Management Inquiry* — qualitative theory-building traditions.
- Langley & Abdallah (2011), "Templates and Turns in Qualitative Studies of Strategy and Management," *Research Methodology in Strategy and Management* — qualitative templates.
- (2017), "Being Where? Navigating the Involvement Paradox in Qualitative Research Accounts," *Organizational Research Methods* — researcher involvement in qualitative fieldwork.

**Would referee well:** process research, longitudinal qualitative case studies, qualitative theory-building, temporality, qualitative rigor and researcher positioning.
