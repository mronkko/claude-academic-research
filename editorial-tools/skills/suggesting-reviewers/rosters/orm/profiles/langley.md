# Ann Langley — HEC Montréal, Canada

Leading qualitative and process-research methodologist (h-index 63, ~28k
citations). Her "Strategies for Theorizing from Process Data" is a foundational
methods text, and she continues to publish on qualitative research quality,
design, and writing — including a 2026 book with Cloutier and Corley.

**Representative method papers:**
- Langley (1999), "Strategies for Theorizing from Process Data," *Academy of Management Review* — canonical comparison of process-data analysis strategies.
- Langley, Smallman, Tsoukas & Van de Ven (2013), "Process Studies of Change in Organization and Management: Unveiling Temporality, Activity, and Flow," *Academy of Management Journal* — ontology and methods of process studies.
- Langley & Abdallah (2011), "Templates and Turns in Qualitative Studies of Strategy and Management," *Research Methodology in Strategy and Management* — epistemological templates for qualitative research.
- Gehman, Glaser, Eisenhardt, Gioia, Langley & Corley (2017), "Finding Theory–Method Fit: A Comparison of Three Qualitative Approaches to Theory Building," *Journal of Management Inquiry*.

**Would referee well:** process research and longitudinal qualitative
designs, theorizing from qualitative data, qualitative rigor/quality and
writing, debates on qualitative templates, case studies of strategic change,
strategy-as-practice and healthcare organizations.
