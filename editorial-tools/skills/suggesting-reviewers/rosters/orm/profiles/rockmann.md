# Kevin Rockmann — George Mason University, USA

Senior organizational-behavior scholar who works across methods: inductive
qualitative field studies (e.g., Pratt, Rockmann & Kaufmann 2006, *AMJ*, on
professional identity; Rockmann & Pratt 2015, *AMD*), laboratory experiments
on cooperation and social dilemmas (*OBHDP*), and multi-study designs. His
explicit methods contributions concern writing up qualitative findings and
the value of exploratory research; he has an editorial role with *Academy of
Management Discoveries*.

**Representative method papers:**
- Rockmann & Vough (2023), "Using Quotes to Present Claims: Practices for the Writing Stages of Qualitative Research," *Organizational Research Methods* — practices for pre-writing and composing qualitative findings (FWCI ~65).
- (2024), "An Application of Large Language Models to Coding Negotiation Transcripts," arXiv preprint — LLM-based coding of transcript data (co-authored).
- (2022), "The Personal Argument for Making Exploratory Research Part of Your Research Portfolio," *Academy of Management Discoveries*; and (2023) "Embracing an Exploratory Mindset: How AMD Is Changing the Script of Good Science," *AMD* — essays on exploratory/abductive research.
- Moliterno & Rockmann (2025), "Touching Ice: Sports Data and Exploratory Research," *AMD*.

**Would referee well:** qualitative-writing and presentation practices,
inductive and exploratory/abductive research designs, use of quotes and
evidence in qualitative papers, LLM-assisted coding of conversational data,
experimental studies of cooperation/trust, identity and relationships
research.

**Notes:** Methods orientation is genuinely mixed (qualitative field studies
plus experiments); his formal method contributions lean qualitative /
exploratory. 2025 AoM symposium on abductive multi-modal (NLP, facial/vocal
cue) analysis of conflict suggests growing computational interest. Not a
psychometrics or advanced-statistics reviewer.
