# Kevin W. Rockmann — George Mason University, USA

Senior organizational-behavior scholar whose substantive work spans identity,
workplace relationships, and remote work, using both qualitative/inductive
designs and experiments and surveys. Methods contributions centre on
qualitative writing and exploratory/abductive research; Rockmann has an editorial
background at Academy of Management Discoveries.

**Representative method papers:**
- Rockmann & Vough (2023), "Using Quotes to Present Claims: Practices for the Writing Stages of Qualitative Research," ORM — how to use data quotes to support claims in qualitative manuscripts.
- Moliterno & Rockmann (2025), "Touching Ice: Sports Data and Exploratory Research," Academy of Management Discoveries — sports data as a setting for exploratory research.
- Rockmann (2023), "Embracing an Exploratory Mindset: How AMD Is Changing the Script of Good Science," Academy of Management Discoveries — case for exploratory, phenomenon-driven research.
- George, Wittman & Rockmann (2021), "Transitioning the Study of Role Transitions: From an Attribute-Based to an Experience-Based Approach," Academy of Management Annals — reframes how role transitions are studied.
- Friedman, …, Rockmann, … (2024), "An Application of Large Language Models to Coding Negotiation Transcripts," arXiv preprint — LLM-assisted coding of qualitative transcript data (large multi-author team).

**Would referee well:** writing up and presenting qualitative evidence,
inductive and exploratory/abductive designs, qualitative studies of identity,
relationships, socialization and remote work, LLM-assisted coding of text.

**Notes:** At George Mason since 2006 per OpenAlex (ESCP Business School
appears as a 2024 secondary affiliation). Former editor at Academy of
Management Discoveries (training knowledge). Also publishes quantitative
OB work (OBHDP, JAP, J. of Management), but has made no quantitative methods
contributions, so Rockmann is a qualitative-methods reviewer, not a quant
methodologist.
