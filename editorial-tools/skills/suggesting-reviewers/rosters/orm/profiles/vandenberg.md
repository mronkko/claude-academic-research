# Robert J. Vandenberg — University of Georgia, USA

Past editor of ORM and a leading quantitative methodologist on measurement invariance, structural equation modeling, and longitudinal/latent-growth change analysis. His measurement-invariance review is one of the most cited methods papers in organizational research.

**Representative method papers:**
- Vandenberg & Lance (2000), "A Review and Synthesis of the Measurement Invariance Literature: Suggestions, Practices, and Recommendations for Organizational Research," ORM — measurement invariance.
- Vandenberg (2002), "Toward a Further Understanding of and Improvement in Measurement Invariance Methods and Procedures," ORM — invariance procedures.
- Ployhart & Vandenberg (2009), "Longitudinal Research: The Theory, Design, and Analysis of Change," Journal of Management — longitudinal design.
- Williams, Vandenberg & Edwards (2009), "Structural Equation Modeling in Management Research: A Guide for Improved Analysis," AMA — SEM.

**Would referee well:** measurement invariance, SEM and CFA, longitudinal/latent-growth modeling and change analysis, scale development.
