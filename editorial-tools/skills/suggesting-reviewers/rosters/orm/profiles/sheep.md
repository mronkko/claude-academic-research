identity work and boundary management (with Kreiner & Hollensbe in *AMJ*)
and for discourse-based analyses of paradox and organizational identity.
He has no dedicated methods-journal papers in OpenAlex; his methodological
substance lies in qualitative and discursive analytic approaches.
