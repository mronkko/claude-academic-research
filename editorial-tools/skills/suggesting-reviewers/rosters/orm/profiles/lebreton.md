# James M. LeBreton — Pennsylvania State University, USA

Past editor of ORM and a quantitative methodologist best known for definitive guidance on interrater reliability/agreement and for relative importance / relative weight analysis. Also works on conditional-reasoning (implicit) personality measurement.

**Representative method papers:**
- LeBreton & Senter (2007), "Answers to 20 Questions About Interrater Reliability and Interrater Agreement," ORM — IRR/IRA.
- Johnson & LeBreton (2004), "History and Use of Relative Importance Indices in Organizational Research," ORM — relative importance.
- Tonidandel & LeBreton (2011), "Relative Importance Analysis: A Useful Supplement to Regression Analysis," JBP — relative weights.
- Tonidandel, LeBreton & Johnson (2009), "Determining the statistical significance of relative weights," Psychological Methods — significance testing of relative weights.

**Would referee well:** interrater reliability and agreement, relative importance/relative weight analysis, aggregation and multilevel measurement, conditional-reasoning measurement, meta-analysis, personality assessment.
