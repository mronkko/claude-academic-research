# Sirio Lonati — NEOMA Business School, France

Early-career quantitative methodologist from the Lausanne (Antonakis) causal-
inference tradition. Methods work covers experimental validity, latent
interaction models, sensitivity analysis for omitted variables (ITCV), and
instrumental-variable heterogeneity; substantive work is on culture and
leadership from an evolutionary/ecological perspective.

**Representative method papers:**
- Lonati, Quiroga, Zehnder & Antonakis (2018), "On doing relevant and rigorous experiments: Review and recommendations," *Journal of Operations Management*.
- Lonati, Rönkkö & Antonakis (2024), "Normality assumption in latent interaction models," *Psychological Methods* — robustness of LMS (Mplus XWITH) to nonnormality.
- Lonati & Wulff (2024), "Hic Sunt Dracones: On the Risks of Comparing the ITCV With Control Variable Correlations," *Journal of Management*.
- Wulff, Sajons, Pogrebna, Lonati, Bastardoz et al. (2023), "Common methodological mistakes," *The Leadership Quarterly*.

**Would referee well:** endogeneity and causal identification, instrumental
variables, omitted-variable sensitivity analysis, laboratory/field experiments,
latent interaction / moderated SEM, cross-cultural and leadership studies using
observational data.

**Notes:** Quantitative orientation. Recent AOM 2025–2026 papers on IV
estimation under effect heterogeneity/LATE (one with Mikko Rönkkö) and on the
ITCV with robust standard errors signal ongoing causal-inference work not yet in
journals. Conflict-of-interest flag: co-author of Mikko Rönkkö (2024
*Psychological Methods*; 2026 AOM paper).
