# Larry J. Williams — University of Nebraska-Lincoln, USA

Founding editor of ORM and an eminent quantitative methodologist in structural equation modeling, confirmatory factor analysis, and the measurement and control of common method variance. Long-running program on latent-variable model evaluation and method-bias techniques.

**Representative method papers:**
- Williams, Hartman & Cavazotte (2010), "Method Variance and Marker Variables: A Review and Comprehensive CFA Marker Technique," ORM — CFA marker technique.
- Podsakoff, Podsakoff, Williams, Huang & Yang (2023), "Common Method Bias: It's Bad, It's Complex, It's Widespread...," ARORG — CMV.
- Williams, Hanna & Smith (2025), "Methods and Theory for Using Parcels in Management Research," Journal of Management — item parceling.
- Williams, Castille & Culpepper (2026), "In defense of the RMSEA-P for latent variable model evaluation," JMSR — SEM fit indices.

**Would referee well:** SEM and CFA, common method variance and marker-variable techniques, latent-variable model fit (RMSEA-P, parcels), causal model evaluation.

**Notes:** ORM founding editor; OpenAlex last-known affiliation is Texas Tech (he was at Nebraska-Lincoln 2017-18 and the member roster lists Nebraska). Deeply quantitative; not a qualitative reviewer.
