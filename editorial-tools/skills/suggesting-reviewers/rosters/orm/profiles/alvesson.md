# Mats Alvesson — Lund University, Sweden

Eminent qualitative/critical methodologist; author of foundational texts on reflexive methodology and the reflexive interview. Reviews qualitative design, interpretive/critical research, discourse analysis, and theory-building-from-data with exceptional authority.

**Representative method papers:**
- Alvesson & Sköldberg (2000/2018), *Reflexive Methodology: New Vistas for Qualitative Research* — foundational qualitative-methods text.
- Alvesson (2003), "Beyond Neopositivists, Romantics, and Localists: A Reflexive Approach to Interviews in Organizational Research," *Academy of Management Review* — qualitative interviewing.
- Alvesson & Kärreman (2007), "Constructing mystery: Empirical matters in theory development," *Academy of Management Review* — abductive theory development.
- Alvesson & Kärreman (2000), "Varieties of Discourse: On the Study of Organizations through Discourse Analysis," *Human Relations* — discourse-analytic method.

**Would referee well:** reflexive and interpretive qualitative methodology, critical management studies, interview-based research, organizational discourse analysis, abductive theory-building.
