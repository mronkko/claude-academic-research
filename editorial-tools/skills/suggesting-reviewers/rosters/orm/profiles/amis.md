# John Amis — University of Edinburgh, UK

Qualitative organizational scholar known for longitudinal, real-time case research on change and for writing on the philosophy and politics of qualitative research quality. Reviews qualitative design, processual/longitudinal case studies, and interpretive rigor with authority.

**Representative method papers:**
- Amis & Silk (2008), "The Philosophy and Politics of Quality in Qualitative Organizational Research," *Organizational Research Methods* — flagship qualitative-quality contribution.
- Amis, Slack & Hinings (2004), "The Pace, Sequence, and Linearity of Radical Change," *Academy of Management Journal* — 12-year real-time longitudinal case design.
- Amis, Slack & Hinings (2002), "Values and Organizational Change," *Journal of Applied Behavioral Science* — longitudinal qualitative change study.

**Would referee well:** qualitative research quality and rigor, longitudinal/processual case-study design, organizational change methods, institutional and inequality fieldwork.

**Notes:** Qualitative orientation; one ORM methods paper makes him a strong on-topic reviewer for qualitative submissions. Not a quantitative reviewer.
