# Paula O'Kane — University of Otago, New Zealand

Qualitative methodologist working at the intersection of HRM and qualitative
research methods. Mid-career (h-index 14; ~20 years active). Best known
methodologically for advancing transparency and trustworthiness in inductive
qualitative analysis and the use of CAQDAS software.

**Representative method papers:**
- O'Kane, Smith & Lerman (2019), "Building Transparency and Trustworthiness in Inductive Research Through Computer-Aided Qualitative Data Analysis Software," *Organizational Research Methods* — flagship qualitative-methods contribution (CAQDAS, inductive rigor).
- O'Kane, Saunders, Blumenfeld & Parker (2024), "Research methodologies for work, workplaces and employment," *Labour & Industry* (special issue editorial) — engaged/impactful methods.
- O'Kane & Hargie (2006), "Intentional and unintentional consequences of substituting face-to-face with e-mail," *Interacting with Computers* — interpretative/qualitative interview methodology.

**Would referee well:** qualitative data analysis and coding, CAQDAS/NVivo
workflows, transparency and trustworthiness in inductive/interpretive
research, interview and thematic-analysis designs, qualitative rigor in
organizational and HRM research.

**Notes:** Substantive footprint is HRM/HRD, but the methodological identity
is firmly qualitative. Do not route purely quantitative/psychometric papers
here. Mid-career — a good "less-obvious" qualitative reviewer.
