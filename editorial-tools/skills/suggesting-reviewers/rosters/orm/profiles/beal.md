# Daniel J. Beal — Virginia Tech, USA

Quantitative methodologist; a primary authority on experience-sampling / ecological momentary assessment and intensive longitudinal, within-person modeling in organizational research. Reviews ESM/diary designs, multilevel and dynamic SEM, and within-person analysis with high authority.

**Representative method papers:**
- Beal (2015), "ESM 2.0: State of the Art and Future Potential of Experience Sampling Methods in Organizational Research," *Annual Review of Org. Psych. and OB* — definitive ESM review.
- Gabriel, Podsakoff, Beal, Scott & Sonnentag (2018), "Experience Sampling Methods: ... Critical Trends and Considerations," *Organizational Research Methods* — ESM best practice.
- Beal & Weiss (2003), "Methods of Ecological Momentary Assessment in Organizational Research," *Organizational Research Methods* — foundational EMA methods.
- Landis, Beal & Tesluk (2000), "A Comparison of Approaches to Forming Composite Measures in Structural Equation Models," *Organizational Research Methods* — measurement/SEM.

**Would referee well:** experience sampling / EMA and diary designs, intensive longitudinal and within-person multilevel modeling, dynamic SEM, affect-dynamics measurement, composite formation.
