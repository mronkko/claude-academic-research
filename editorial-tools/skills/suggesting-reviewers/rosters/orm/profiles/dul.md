# Jan Dul — RSM Erasmus University Rotterdam, Netherlands

Methodologist; originator of Necessary Condition Analysis (NCA) and a methods authority on necessity logic, case-study methodology, and theory testing.

**Representative method papers:**
- Dul (2015), "Necessary Condition Analysis (NCA)," Organizational Research Methods — new technique.
- Dul, van der Laan & Kuik (2018), "A Statistical Significance Test for Necessary Condition Analysis," ORM — inferential test for NCA.
- Dul (2015), "Identifying single necessary conditions with NCA and fsQCA," J. Business Research — method comparison.
- Dul & Hak (2007), "Case Study Methodology in Business Research" (book) — theory-testing case study.

**Would referee well:** Necessary Condition Analysis, necessity/sufficiency logic, configurational and QCA methods, case-study design and theory testing.

**Notes:** Core methodologist; NCA work appears repeatedly in ORM. Applies the method across substantive fields (creativity, ergonomics, clinical). Not a qualitative-interpretive reviewer.
