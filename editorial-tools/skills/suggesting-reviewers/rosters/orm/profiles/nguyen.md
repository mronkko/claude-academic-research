# Duc Nguyen — University of Manchester, UK (not listed on board page)

Early-career qualitative methodologist (Alliance Manchester Business School)
whose work critically examines how technology, from CAQDAS to generative AI,
shapes qualitative data analysis in management and organization studies. His
2025 ORM paper with Catherine Welch is already highly cited (FWCI ~84),
making him a current, on-topic reviewer for GenAI-in-qualitative-research
submissions.

**Representative method papers:**
- Nguyen & Welch (2025), "Generative Artificial Intelligence in Qualitative Data Analysis: Analyzing—Or Just Chatting?," *Organizational Research Methods* — technologically informed critique of LLMs for qualitative coding and thematic analysis; argues uncritical adoption introduces unacceptable epistemic risks.
- Nguyen, Aguzzoli & Welch (2026), "Technology and the future of qualitative data analysis," *Organization Studies* — SCOT-based analysis of CAQDAS; problematizes "indexical coding" as threatening interpretive agency and methodological pluralism.

**Would referee well:** submissions using or evaluating LLMs/GenAI for
qualitative coding or analysis, CAQDAS-based analysis workflows, debates on
coding vs. interpretation in qualitative research, reflexivity and
transparency in technology-assisted qualitative methods.
