# Sara R. S. T. A. Elias — University of Victoria, Canada

Qualitative-methods scholar (h-index 10, citations rising quickly: 2-year mean
citedness ~8.7) who works on the craft of qualitative research: gaining
access, reflexivity, interviewing, and resisting the "objectification" of
qualitative research. Her substantive work uses ethnography and abductive
process models to study entrepreneurship, aesthetics, and arts and craft
entrepreneurs.
