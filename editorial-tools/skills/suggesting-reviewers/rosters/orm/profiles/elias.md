# Sara R. S. T. A. Elias — University of Victoria, Canada

Sara Elias is a qualitative organization and entrepreneurship scholar whose substantive work examines arts, craft, and creative-industry entrepreneurs — aesthetics, imagination, embodiment, and identity — using interpretive, process-oriented designs. Alongside that program she contributes to qualitative methodology itself: access and informant recruitment, the long interview, reflexivity in research pairs, and a critique of the "objectification" of qualitative research that sidelines context, the researcher, and non-interview data. Publishes in *Organization Studies*, *Organization*, *Organization Theory*, *Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal*, *JMI*, and ORM.

**Representative method papers:**
- Peticca-Harris, deGama & Elias (2016), "A Dynamic Process Model for Finding Informants and Gaining Access in Qualitative Research," *Organizational Research Methods* — process model of recruitment and access as an ongoing, negotiated activity.
- Hansen, Elias, Stevenson, Smith, Alexander & Barros (2023), "Resisting the Objectification of Qualitative Research: The Unsilencing of Context, Researchers, and Noninterview Data," *Organizational Research Methods* — critique of template-driven, interview-centric qualitative work.
- Crawford, Chiles & Elias (2020), "Long Interviews in Organizational Research: Unleashing the Power of 'Show and Tell'," *Journal of Management Inquiry* — long-interview technique with artifacts and demonstration.
- Duncan & Elias (2020), "(Inter)subjectivity in the research pair: Countertransference and radical reflexivity in organizational research," *Organization* — reflexivity in co-researcher fieldwork.
- deGama, Elias & Peticca-Harris (2019), "The good academic: re-imagining good research in organization and management studies," *Qualitative Research in Organizations and Management* — research-quality norms in qualitative OMS.
- Peticca-Harris, Elias & Owens-Jackson (2026), "Using Photovoice Methods to Illustrate Occupational Health and Safety Risks in Flexible Work," handbook chapter — photovoice as a visual participatory method.
