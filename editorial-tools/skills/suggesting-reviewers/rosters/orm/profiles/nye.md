# Chris Nye — Michigan State University, USA

Quantitative psychometric methodologist. Central contributions are effect-size indices for measurement equivalence (dMACS), model-fit evaluation in CFA, and scale development in personality and vocational interests.

**Representative method papers:**
- Nye & Drasgow (2011), "Effect size indices for analyses of measurement equivalence," JAP — dMACS effect size for nonequivalence.
- Nye, Bradburn, Olenick, Bialko & Drasgow (2018), "How big are my effects? Examining the magnitude of effect sizes in studies of measurement equivalence," ORM — effect-size benchmarks.
- Nye & Drasgow (2010), "Assessing goodness of fit: Simple rules of thumb simply do not work," ORM — CFA fit indices.
- Zhang, Highhouse & Nye (2018), "Development and validation of the General Risk Propensity Scale (GRiPS)," JBDM — scale development.

**Would referee well:** Measurement invariance/equivalence and effect sizes, CFA and model fit, scale construction and validation, IRT-based personality/interest assessment.

**Notes:** Methodologist publishing in ORM, JAP, and applied-psychology outlets. Not a qualitative reviewer.
