# Anne D. Smith — University of Tennessee, USA

Senior qualitative methodologist in management/organization studies. A leading voice on transparency and rigor in inductive research, CAQDAS, visual/photographic methods, and qualitative templates; multiple ORM methods papers and an ORM feature-topic editor.

**Representative method papers:**
- O'Kane, Smith & Lerman (2019), "Building Transparency and Trustworthiness in Inductive Research Through CAQDAS," ORM — qualitative analysis software/rigor.
- Ray & Smith (2011), "Using Photographs to Research Organizations," ORM — visual/photographic methods.
- Köhler, Smith & Bhakoo (2021), "Templates in Qualitative Research Methods: Origins, Limitations, and New Directions," ORM — qualitative templates.
- Hymer & Smith (2024), "Making Exceptions Exceptional: A Cross-Methodological Review," J. of Management — handling anomalies across qual/quant.

**Would referee well:** inductive/qualitative design and rigor, CAQDAS use and transparency, visual methods, qualitative templates and theorizing, strategy-as-practice, case-study methodology.

**Notes:** Predominantly qualitative; one of ORM's core qualitative-methods reviewers. Not a quantitative/psychometrics reviewer.
