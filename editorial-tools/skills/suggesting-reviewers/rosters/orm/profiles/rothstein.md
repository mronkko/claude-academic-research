# Hannah R. Rothstein — Baruch College CUNY, USA

Eminent meta-analysis and research-synthesis methodologist. Co-author of the field-defining "Introduction to Meta-Analysis" and the standard reference on publication bias; central contributions to fixed/random-effects modeling, heterogeneity interpretation, and synthesis power.

**Representative method papers:**
- Borenstein, Hedges, Higgins & Rothstein (2009/2021), Introduction to Meta-Analysis (Wiley) — the standard methods text.
- Rothstein, Sutton & Borenstein (2005), Publication Bias in Meta-Analysis (Wiley) — publication-bias methodology.
- Borenstein, Hedges, Higgins & Rothstein (2010), "A basic introduction to fixed-effect and random-effects models for meta-analysis," Research Synthesis Methods — model choice.
- Borenstein, Higgins, Hedges & Rothstein (2017), "Basics of meta-analysis: I-squared is not an absolute measure of heterogeneity," RSM — heterogeneity interpretation.

**Would referee well:** Meta-analysis and research synthesis, publication bias, fixed/random-effects modeling, heterogeneity, systematic-review methodology and synthesis power.

**Notes:** Core meta-analytic methodologist. Not a qualitative reviewer.
