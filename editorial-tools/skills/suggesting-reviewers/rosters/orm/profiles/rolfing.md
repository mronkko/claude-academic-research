# Ingo Rolfing — University of Passau, Germany

Political-science methodologist and a leading authority on case studies,
process tracing, QCA and set-theoretic multi-method research (88 of his
OpenAlex works are tagged QCA). Much of his work appears in *Sociological
Methods & Research*; recent work extends to research transparency,
preanalysis plans for qualitative research, and LLM-assisted literature
review.

**Representative method papers:**
- Schneider & Rohlfing (2013), "Combining QCA and Process Tracing in Set-Theoretic Multi-Method Research," *Sociological Methods & Research* — principles for case selection after QCA.
- Rohlfing (2012), *Case Studies and Causal Inference: An Integrative Framework*, Palgrave Macmillan.
- Rohlfing & Schneider (2016), "A Unifying Framework for Causal Analysis in Set-Theoretic Multimethod Research," *Sociological Methods & Research*.
- Rohlfing (2026), "Identification Requirements for Residual-based Case Selection in Mixed-methods Research," *Field Methods*.

**Would referee well:** QCA / fuzzy-set and set-theoretic analysis, case
selection and nested analysis, process tracing and Bayesian reasoning in
qualitative inference, multi-method research designs, transparency and
preregistration of qualitative research, LLM-based coding of methods
literatures.
