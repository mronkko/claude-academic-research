# Joep Cornelissen — Rotterdam School of Management, Erasmus University, Netherlands

Senior organization theorist (h-index 61, ~14k citations; Erasmus since 2015,
also University of Liverpool). His methodological authority is in theorizing:
how theory papers are built (propositions vs. process models vs. typologies),
how metaphor and analogy work in theory, and what kinds of inference a research
approach can support. Empirically he works with qualitative, discursive, and
communication data.

**Representative method papers:**
- Cornelissen (2017), "Editor's Comments: Developing Propositions, a Process Model, or a Typology? Addressing the Challenges of Writing Theory Without a Boilerplate," *Academy of Management Review*: widely used guide to theorizing styles (OpenAlex dates it 2016-07).
- Cornelissen (2005), "Beyond Compare: Metaphor in Organization Theory," *Academy of Management Review*: domains-interaction model of metaphor in theorizing.
- Cornelissen & Werner (2014), "Putting Framing in Perspective: A Review of Framing and Frame Analysis across the Management and Organizational Literature," *Academy of Management Annals*: conceptual and analytic integration of frame analysis.
- Clarke, Cornelissen & Healey (2026), "Is the Gene-ie Out of the Bottle? The Problems and Perils of Genetics Research in Management," *Journal of Management Studies*: critique of the epistemology and inference behind genetics-based management research.

**Would referee well:** theory-development and theorizing-method papers, use of
metaphor/analogy in theorizing, framing and discourse analysis, qualitative
process and configurational theorizing (see Meurer, Cornelissen & Torres 2026
*AMR* commentary on configurational theorizing), critiques of research
epistemology and inference, and AI/LLMs in theory production (his 2026
*Organization Studies* essay).

**Notes:** Theory- and qualitative-oriented. Not a reviewer for
statistical-estimation papers, though he can judge inferential claims at the
epistemological level. Former AMR associate editor and JMS general editor (from
training knowledge). OpenAlex lists both Erasmus University Rotterdam and the
University of Liverpool as current. Three tiny Cornelissen records (1–3 works)
are fragments or namesakes and were excluded.
