# Joep Cornelissen — RSM Erasmus University, The Netherlands

Senior organization theorist and communication scholar (Erasmus University Rotterdam since 2015; earlier at Leeds, VU Amsterdam and Amsterdam). He works on metaphor, framing, sensemaking, entrepreneurial cognition and corporate communication, and writes widely on how management scholars theorize. His methodological work is conceptual and qualitative: whether qualitative research should follow templates to be rigorous, how to write theory (propositions, process models, typologies), what causal mechanisms are, construct fallacies, and protocols for analyzing gesture in natural settings. Three ORM articles; former AMR associate editor and JMS general editor (training knowledge).

**Representative method papers:**
- Harley & Cornelissen (2020), "Rigor With or Without Templates? The Pursuit of Methodological Rigor in Qualitative Research," ORM — critique of template-driven qualitative rigor.
- Cornelissen & Werner (2025), "What Are Mechanisms? Ways of Conceptualizing and Studying Causal Mechanisms," ORM — compares ways of conceptualizing and studying mechanisms.
- Clarke, Llewellyn, Cornelissen & Viney (2019), "Gesture Analysis and Organizational Research: The Development and Application of a Protocol for Naturalistic Settings," ORM — a protocol for multimodal/gesture data.
- Cornelissen (2016), "Preserving Theoretical Divergence in Management Research: Why the Explanatory Potential of Qualitative Research Should Be Harnessed Rather than Suppressed," J. of Management Studies — the explanatory role of qualitative research.
- Cornelissen (2016), "Editor's Comments: Developing Propositions, a Process Model, or a Typology? Addressing the Challenges of Writing Theory Without a Boilerplate," Academy of Management Review — forms of theorizing.
- Cornelissen, Werner, De Schutter & Solinger (2026), "Construct Fallacies and Construct Validation in Theories of Management and Organization," Academy of Management Review — construct validation at the level of theory.
- Cornelissen & Werner (2014), "Putting Framing in Perspective: A Review of Framing and Frame Analysis across the Management and Organizational Literature," Academy of Management Annals — framing and frame analysis.
