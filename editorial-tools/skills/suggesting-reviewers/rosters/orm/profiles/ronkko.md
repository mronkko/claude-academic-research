# Mikko Rönkkö — University of Jyväskylä, Finland

Mikko Rönkkö is a quantitative methodologist working in management, information systems, and entrepreneurship research. His program centers on structural equation modeling and the critical evaluation of latent-variable and composite-based techniques — most prominently a sustained, influential critique of partial least squares (PLS) path modeling — alongside measurement quality, reliability, discriminant validity, control-variable practice, and endogeneity in multilevel models. His work leans heavily on Monte Carlo simulation to demonstrate the statistical behaviour of estimators and to ground methodological recommendations. He sits on the *Organizational Research Methods* editorial team as an Associate Editor (formerly at Aalto University; now at the University of Jyväskylä). His most-cited article, the 2020 discriminant-validity guideline (1,100+ citations), is an ORM staple.

**Representative method papers:**
- Rönkkö & Cho (2020), "An Updated Guideline for Assessing Discriminant Validity," *Organizational Research Methods* — generalized definition of discriminant validity and an updated assessment procedure; his flagship contribution.
- Rönkkö & Evermann (2013), "A Critical Examination of Common Beliefs About Partial Least Squares Path Modeling," *Organizational Research Methods* — foundational critique dismantling widespread misconceptions about PLS capabilities.
- Rönkkö, McIntosh, Antonakis & Edwards (2016), "Partial Least Squares Path Modeling: Time for Some Serious Second Thoughts," *Journal of Operations Management* — non-technical review plus simulation demonstrating PLS's intractable problems.
- Antonakis, Bastardoz & Rönkkö (2019), "On Ignoring the Random Effects Assumption in Multilevel Models," *Organizational Research Methods* — endogeneity in multilevel models and a cluster-mean correction, supported by large-scale simulation.
- Aguirre-Urreta & Rönkkö (2018), "Statistical Inference with PLSc Using Bootstrap Confidence Intervals," *MIS Quarterly* — bootstrap-based inference for consistent PLS.
- Rönkkö, Maula & Wennberg (2025), "Qualitative Comparative Analysis in Entrepreneurship Research," *Entrepreneurship Theory and Practice* — editorial reviewing false-positive risks and pitfalls of QCA.

**Would referee well:** structural equation modeling and CFA; PLS / composite-based SEM and its limitations; measurement, reliability, and discriminant/convergent validity; common-method variance; control-variable selection; endogeneity and multilevel modeling; Monte Carlo simulation studies of estimator behaviour; QCA critique.
