# Rebecca Piekkari — Aalto University, Finland

Leading qualitative methodologist in international business and management. Central contributions concern case-study methodology, qualitative interviewing of corporate elites, and cross-language/translation issues in qualitative research.

**Representative method papers:**
- Welch, Piekkari, Plakoyiannaki & Paavilainen-Mäntymäki (2010), "Theorising from case studies: Towards a pluralist future for IB research," JIBS — case-study theorizing typology.
- Piekkari, Welch & Paavilainen (2008), "The case study as disciplinary convention," ORM — review of case-study practice.
- Welch & Piekkari (2006), "Crossing language boundaries: Qualitative interviewing in international business," MIR — cross-language interviewing.
- Welch, Piekkari, Penttinen & Tahvanainen (2002), "Corporate elites as informants in qualitative IB research," IBR — elite interviewing.
