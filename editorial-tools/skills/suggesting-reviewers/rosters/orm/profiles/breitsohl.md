# Heiko Breitsohl — University of Klagenfurt, Austria

Quantitative methodologist in organizational behavior; works on SEM-based analysis of experiments, careless-responding detection, and questionnaire scale design, alongside substantive work on commitment and presenteeism. Reviews experimental SEM, measurement, and survey-methodology submissions with authority.

**Representative method papers:**
- Breitsohl (2018), "Beyond ANOVA: An Introduction to Structural Equation Models for Experimental Designs," *Organizational Research Methods* — SEM for experiments.
- Breitsohl & Steidelmüller (2017), "The Impact of Insufficient Effort Responding Detection Methods on Substantive Responses," *Applied Psychology* — careless-responding/measurement.
- Hoogeveen, Sarafoglou, ... Breitsohl et al. (2022), "A many-analysts approach to the relation between religiosity and well-being," *Religion, Brain & Behavior* — robustness/many-analysts methodology.
