# Robert Wright — Hong Kong Polytechnic University, China

Methodologist in managerial and organizational cognition, best known for advancing the repertory grid technique and personal construct theory as knowledge-elicitation methods for studying strategists' cognition. Long Hong Kong Polytechnic affiliation.

**Representative method papers:**
- Wright (2007), "Eliciting Cognitions of Strategizing Using Advanced Repertory Grids in a World Constructed and Reconstructed," ORM — repertory grid method.
- Wright & Lam (2002), "Comparing Apples with Apples: The Importance of Element Wording in Grid Applications," Journal of Constructivist Psychology — grid methodology.
- Wright (2004), "Mapping cognitions to better understand attitudinal and behavioral responses in appraisal research," JOB — cognitive mapping.
- Hodgkinson, Wright & Anderson (2015), "Emotionalizing Strategy Research with the Repertory Grid Technique," book chapter — grid technique extensions.
