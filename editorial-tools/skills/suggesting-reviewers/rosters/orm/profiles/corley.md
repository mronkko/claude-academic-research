# Kevin Corley — Imperial College London, UK

Qualitative methodologist and co-architect of the "Gioia methodology" for rigorous inductive/grounded theorizing; an authority on qualitative rigor, theory building, and theory-method fit.

**Representative method papers:**
- Gioia, Corley & Hamilton (2012), "Seeking Qualitative Rigor in Inductive Research," Organizational Research Methods — the canonical Gioia-method paper.
- Corley & Gioia (2011), "Building Theory about Theory Building: What Constitutes a Theoretical Contribution?," Academy of Management Review.
- Gehman, Glaser, Eisenhardt, Gioia, Langley & Corley (2017), "Finding Theory-Method Fit: A Comparison of Three Qualitative Approaches to Theory Building," Journal of Management Inquiry.
- Shah & Corley (2006), "Building Better Theory by Bridging the Quantitative-Qualitative Divide," Journal of Management Studies.
