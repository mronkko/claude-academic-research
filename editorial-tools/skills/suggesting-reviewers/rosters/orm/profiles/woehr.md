# David J. Woehr — University of North Carolina-Charlotte, USA

Quantitative methodologist in industrial-organizational psychology: meta-analysis, performance appraisal/rating measurement, rater training, and scale development. Long record of methods-heavy meta-analyses and measurement instruments.

**Representative method papers:**
- Woehr & Huffcutt (1994), "Rater training for performance appraisal: A quantitative review," JOOP — meta-analysis / rater training.
- Ohland, Loughry, Woehr et al. (2012), "Comprehensive Assessment of Team Member Effectiveness: Development of a Behaviorally Anchored Rating Scale," AMLE — BARS scale development.
- Miller, Woehr & Hudspeth (2002), "The Meaning and Measurement of Work Ethic," JVB — multidimensional inventory development.
- Meriac, Woehr & Banister (2010), "Generational Differences in Work Ethic: Measurement Equivalence Across Three Cohorts," JBP — measurement invariance.
