# Alina Baluch — University of St Andrews, UK

Mid-career management scholar (h-index 13) working mainly with qualitative
multiple-case designs on HRM implementation and nonprofit/social-care
organizations, with a smaller quantitative strand (longitudinal, multi-source
HRM–performance panels in the NHS). Her methodological contributions are about
theorizing: how case studies build on and position against existing theory, and
how review studies can generate theory instead of only summarizing.

**Representative method papers:**
- Ridder, Hoon & Baluch (2012), "Entering a Dialogue: Positioning Case Study Findings towards Theory," *British Journal of Management*: three ways a theory-building case study can position its contribution against prior theory.
- Hoon & Baluch (2019), "The Role of Dialectical Interrogation in Review Studies: Theorizing from What We See Rather Than What We Have Already Seen," *Journal of Management Studies*: theorizing strategies (similarities and anomalies) for review articles.
- Baluch & Ridder (2020), "Mapping the Research Landscape of Strategic Human Resource Management in Nonprofit Organizations: A Systematic Review and Avenues for Future Research," *Nonprofit and Voluntary Sector Quarterly*: applied systematic-review method (74 articles).

**Would referee well:** theory-building case studies and multiple-case designs,
positioning qualitative contributions, review and integrative-synthesis
methodology (theorizing in reviews, systematic reviews), qualitative process
studies of HRM implementation; substantive fit for nonprofit/public-sector
settings.
