# Catherine Cassell — Durham University, UK

Leading qualitative methodologist in organizational and management research; co-author of the foundational handbooks of qualitative organizational methods and author of work on evaluation criteria, reflexivity, and the research interview.

**Representative method papers:**
- Cassell & Symon (2004), "Essential Guide to Qualitative Methods in Organizational Research," SAGE.
- Johnson, Buehring, Cassell & Symon (2006), "Evaluating qualitative management research: towards a contingent criteriology," International Journal of Management Reviews.
- Cassell & Johnson (2006), "Action research: Explaining the diversity," Human Relations.
- Nadin & Cassell (2006), "The use of a research diary as a tool for reflexive practice," QRAM.
- Cassell, Cunliffe & Grandy (2018), "The SAGE Handbook of Qualitative Business and Management Research Methods."

**Would referee well:** qualitative methods design and evaluation, action research, reflexivity, interviewing, template analysis, interpretivist/constructionist studies.
