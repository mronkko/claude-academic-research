# Boris B. Baltes — Wayne State University, USA

Quantitative I-O psychologist; prolific meta-analyst across work-family, climate, workaholism, and group-decision domains, with attention to construct measurement in work-and-aging research. Reviews meta-analytic and survey-based quantitative submissions with authority.

**Representative method papers:**
- Michel, Kotrba, Mitchelson, Clark & Baltes (2010), "Antecedents of work–family conflict: A meta-analytic review," *Journal of Organizational Behavior* — large-scale meta-analysis.
- Parker, Baltes et al. (2003), "Relationships between psychological climate perceptions and work outcomes: a meta-analytic review," *Journal of Organizational Behavior* — meta-analysis and construct clarification.
- Baltes, Briggs, Huff, Wright & Neuman (1999), "Flexible and compressed workweek schedules: A meta-analysis," *Journal of Applied Psychology* — meta-analytic technique.
- Fasbender, Baltes & Rudolph (2022), "New Directions for Measurement in the Field of Work, Aging and Retirement," *Work, Aging and Retirement* — measurement review.

**Would referee well:** meta-analysis (methods and applications), publication-bias assessment, construct measurement, survey and longitudinal designs in I-O / work-family research.

**Notes:** Substantive I-O scholar whose method strength is meta-analysis and measurement; not a qualitative reviewer.
