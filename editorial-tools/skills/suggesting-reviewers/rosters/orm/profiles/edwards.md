# Jeffrey R. Edwards — University of North Carolina, Chapel Hill, USA

Eminent quantitative methodologist. Foundational work on congruence/fit modeling (polynomial regression and response surface analysis), construct-measurement relationships, and moderated mediation.

**Representative method papers:**
- Edwards & Lambert (2007), "Methods for integrating moderation and mediation," Psychological Methods — moderated path analysis.
- Edwards & Bagozzi (2000), "On the nature and direction of relationships between constructs and measures," Psychological Methods — formative/reflective measurement.
- Edwards & Parry (1993), "On the use of polynomial regression equations as an alternative to difference scores," AMJ — congruence/fit modeling.
- Edwards (2001), "Multidimensional Constructs in Organizational Behavior Research," ORM — construct representation.

**Would referee well:** congruence/fit and polynomial/response-surface modeling, construct validity and measurement theory, moderated mediation, response validity and survey data quality.

**Notes:** Core quantitative methodologist; flagship author for ORM and Psychological Methods. Not a qualitative reviewer.
