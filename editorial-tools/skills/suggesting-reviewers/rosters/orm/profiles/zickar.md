# Michael J. Zickar — Bowling Green State University, USA

Quantitative psychometrician in industrial-organizational psychology, best known for item response theory applications to detecting faking on personality tests, mixed-model IRT, and work on sampling/data quality. Also writes on the history of methods.

**Representative method papers:**
- Zickar & Robie (1999), "Modeling faking good on personality items: An item-level analysis," JAP — IRT/faking.
- Zickar, Gibby & Robie (2004), "Uncovering Faking Samples in Applicant, Incumbent, and Experimental Data Sets: An Application of Mixed-Model Item Response Theory," ORM — mixed-model IRT.
- Dalal & Zickar (2011), "Some Common Myths About Centering Predictor Variables in Moderated Multiple Regression and Polynomial Regression," ORM — centering/moderated regression.
- Zickar & Keith (2022), "Innovations in Sampling: Improving the Appropriateness and Quality of Samples in Organizational Research," ARORG — sampling/data quality.
