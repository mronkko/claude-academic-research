# Paul Bliese — University of South Carolina, USA

Past editor of ORM and a leading quantitative methodologist in multilevel modeling for organizational data: within-group agreement and ICC estimation, random-coefficient/growth models, and the treatment of nonindependence in grouped data. Substantive work on military stress/PTSD underpins the methods record.

**Representative method papers:**
- Bliese (2000), "Within-group agreement, non-independence, and reliability: Implications for data aggregation and analysis," book chapter — agreement/ICC.
- Bliese & Ployhart (2002), "Growth Modeling Using Random Coefficient Models," ORM — growth/random-coefficient modeling.
- Bliese & Hanges (2004), "Being Both Too Liberal and Too Conservative: The Perils of Treating Grouped Data as though They Were Independent," ORM — nonindependence.
- Maltarich, Bliese & Carpenter (2026), "Relationships Between Shared Group Properties: Theory, Measurement, Estimation, and Adjustment," Personnel Psychology — group-mean associations.

**Would referee well:** multilevel/random-coefficient modeling, within-group agreement and ICCs, growth and longitudinal models, aggregation and nonindependence, R-based multilevel analysis.

**Notes:** ORM past editor. Deeply quantitative multilevel methodologist (author of the R multilevel package). Not a qualitative reviewer.
