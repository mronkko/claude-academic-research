# John Antonakis — University of Lausanne, Switzerland

Leading quantitative methodologist on causal inference and endogeneity in management/leadership research, alongside leadership measurement and field/lab experiments. Reviews endogeneity, IV/2SLS, causal identification, measurement, and experimental work with high authority.

**Representative method papers:**
- Antonakis (2017), "On doing better science: From thrill of discovery to policy implications," *The Leadership Quarterly* — causal inference / endogeneity manifesto.
- Antonakis, Avolio & Sivasubramaniam (2003), "Context and leadership: ... full-range leadership theory using the MLQ," *The Leadership Quarterly* — leadership measurement/validation.
- Antonakis, Bastardoz, Jacquart & Shamir (2016), "Charisma: An Ill-Defined and Ill-Measured Gift," *Annual Review of Org. Psych. and OB* — measurement critique and correct model specification.
- Antonakis & House (2014), "Instrumental leadership: Measurement and extension of transformational–transactional leadership theory," *The Leadership Quarterly* — scale measurement.
