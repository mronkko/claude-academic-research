# John R. Busenbark — University of Notre Dame, USA

Quantitative strategy methodologist known for influential methods papers on sample-selection (Heckman) models, ratio variables, and Monte Carlo evaluation of techniques, alongside substantive corporate-governance/strategic-leadership work. Reviews selection bias, simulation-based method evaluation, and econometric design with high authority.

**Representative method papers:**
- Certo, Busenbark, Woo & Semadeni (2015), "Sample selection bias and Heckman models in strategic management research," *Strategic Management Journal* — Heckman/selection-bias guidance.
- Certo, Busenbark, Kalm & LePine (2018), "Divided We Fall: How Ratios Undermine Research in Strategic Management," *Organizational Research Methods* — ratio-variable critique via simulation.
- Certo, Busenbark, Li & Withers (2026), "A primer on using Monte Carlo simulations to evaluate research methods in strategic management," *Strategic Management Review* — Monte Carlo methodology.
