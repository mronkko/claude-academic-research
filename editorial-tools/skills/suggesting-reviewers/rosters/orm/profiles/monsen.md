- Monsen, Patzelt & Saxton (2009), "Beyond simple utility: Incentive design and trade-offs for corporate employee-entrepreneurs," ETP — conjoint field experiment.
- Monsen & Boss (2008), "The impact of strategic entrepreneurship inside the organization," ETP — multilevel SEM on survey data.
