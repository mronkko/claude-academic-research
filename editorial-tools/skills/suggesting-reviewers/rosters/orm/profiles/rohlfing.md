# Ingo Rohlfing — University of Passau, Germany

Political-science methodologist and a leading figure in set-theoretic
methods (QCA), case-study research, and process tracing, including how to
combine them with cross-case and regression analysis in multimethod designs.
Publishes mainly in Sociological Methods & Research and Field Methods. Senior
(full professor; Cologne, Bremen and Jacobs/Constructor before Passau).

**Representative method papers:**
- Schneider & Rohlfing (2013), "Combining QCA and Process Tracing in Set-Theoretic Multi-Method Research," Sociological Methods & Research — set-theoretic multimethod research framework.
- Rohlfing (2012), *Case Studies and Causal Inference: An Integrative Framework*, Palgrave Macmillan — case-study design and causal inference.
- Schneider & Rohlfing (2014), "Case Studies Nested in Fuzzy-set QCA on Sufficiency," Sociological Methods & Research — case selection after fsQCA.
- Rohlfing (2013), "Comparative Hypothesis Testing Via Process Tracing," Sociological Methods & Research — process tracing as hypothesis testing.
- Rohlfing & Zuber (2019), "Check Your Truth Conditions! Clarifying the Relationship between Theories of Causation and Social Science Methods for Causal Inference," Sociological Methods & Research — causation theories vs. methods.
- Rohlfing (2019), "The Choice between Crisp and Fuzzy Sets in Qualitative Comparative Analysis and the Ambiguous Consequences for Finding Consistent Set Relations," Field Methods — calibration choices in QCA.
- Behrens & Rohlfing (2025), "The Integration of Bayesian Regression Analysis and Bayesian Process Tracing in Mixed-Methods Research," Sociological Methods & Research — Bayesian mixed-methods integration.
- Rohlfing (2025), "An Exploration of the Presence of Positive-Results Bias in Qualitative Comparative Analysis," Sociological Methods & Research — publication bias in QCA.
- Rohlfing (2026), "Identification Requirements for Residual-based Case Selection in Mixed-methods Research," Field Methods — case selection from regression residuals.

**Would referee well:** QCA/fsQCA (calibration, consistency, robustness,
reporting), set-theoretic and configurational methods, case selection,
process tracing (including Bayesian), mixed/multimethod designs, causal
inference from case studies, research transparency, preregistration and
publication bias in qualitative and set-theoretic work.
