# Harry P. Bowen — Queens University of Charlotte, USA

Quantitative methodologist and trade economist; in strategy he is best known for econometric methods papers on limited dependent variables and on the limitations of cross-sectional analysis. Reviews LDV/logit/probit/Tobit, panel modeling, and econometric design with high authority.

**Representative method papers:**
- Wiersema & Bowen (2009), "The use of limited dependent variable techniques in strategy research: issues and methods," *Strategic Management Journal* — LDV methods.
- Bowen & Wiersema (1999), "Matching method to paradigm in strategy research: limitations of cross-sectional analysis and some methodological alternatives," *Strategic Management Journal* — design/estimation critique.
- Bowen (2020), "Limited Dependent Variables in Management Research," *Oxford Research Encyclopedia of Business and Management* — methods reference.
- Bowen (1989), "Empirical methods for international trade," *Journal of International Economics* — trade econometrics.

**Would referee well:** limited dependent variable models, panel/longitudinal and dynamic Tobit estimation, cross-sectional vs. longitudinal design choices, econometrics in strategy and international business.
