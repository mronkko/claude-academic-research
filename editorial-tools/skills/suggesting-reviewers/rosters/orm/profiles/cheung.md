# Gordon W. Cheung — University of Auckland, New Zealand

Quantitative SEM methodologist; author of the most widely cited work on goodness-of-fit cutoffs for measurement invariance, plus foundational papers on latent-variable mediation and validity reporting.

**Representative method papers:**
- Cheung & Rensvold (2002), "Evaluating Goodness-of-Fit Indexes for Testing Measurement Invariance," Structural Equation Modeling.
- Cheung & Lau (2007), "Testing Mediation and Suppression Effects of Latent Variables," Organizational Research Methods.
- Cheung & Lau (2015), "Accuracy of Parameter Estimates and Confidence Intervals in Moderated Mediation Models," ORM.
- Cheung, Cooper-Thomas, Lau & Wang (2023), "Reporting reliability, convergent and discriminant validity with SEM: best-practice recommendations," APJM.
