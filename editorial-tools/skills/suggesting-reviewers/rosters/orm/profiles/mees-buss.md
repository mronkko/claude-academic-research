# Jacqueline Mees-Buss — University of Sydney, Australia

Qualitative methodologist and international business scholar known for critiquing standardized qualitative templates (such as the Gioia methodology) and advocating heuristic, hermeneutic rigor.

**Representative method papers:**
- Mees-Buss, Welch & Piekkari (2020/2022), "From Templates to Heuristics: How and Why to Move Beyond the Gioia Methodology," *Organizational Research Methods* — methodological critique of template-driven qualitative coding.
- Pratt, Sonenshein & Feldman / Mees-Buss et al. (2022), "A Curated Debate: On Using 'Templates' in Qualitative Research," *Journal of Management Inquiry* — qualitative rigor debate.
