# Jacqueline Mees-Buss — University of Sydney, Australia

Interpretive qualitative researcher whose signature methods contribution is the
ORM critique of the Gioia methodology and a proposal to replace templates with
hermeneutic heuristics. Substantive work is on multinational corporations,
managerial ideology, and CSR, typically via qualitative process and textual
analysis.

**Representative method papers:**
- Mees-Buss, Welch & Piekkari (2020), "From Templates to Heuristics: How and Why to Move Beyond the Gioia Methodology," *Organizational Research Methods* — hermeneutic alternative to template-driven inductive research.
- Gioia, Corley, Eisenhardt, Feldman, Langley et al. (2022), "A Curated Debate: On Using 'Templates' in Qualitative Research," *Journal of Management Inquiry* — contributor to the debate.
- Mees-Buss & Welch (2018), "Managerial Ideologies Dividing the Corporate Elite: A process study of the rise and fall of a counter-ideology," *Organization Studies* — methodologically substantive qualitative process study (empirical, not a methods paper).
