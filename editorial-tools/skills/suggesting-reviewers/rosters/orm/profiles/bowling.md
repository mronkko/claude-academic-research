# Nathan Bowling — University of Central Florida, USA

Senior I-O psychologist (h-index 41, ~10.4k citations) who moved from Wright
State to UCF in 2024. Works quantitatively on survey data quality, especially
insufficient effort / careless responding, and on construct validity and
redundancy. He has also written many meta-analyses on job attitudes, stress,
and workplace harassment.

**Representative method papers:**
- Huang, Liu & Bowling (2015), "Insufficient effort responding: Examining an insidious confound in survey data," *Journal of Applied Psychology*: shows inattentive responding can inflate observed correlations.
- Huang, Bowling, Liu & Li (2015), "Detecting Insufficient Effort Responding with an Infrequency Scale: Evaluating Validity and Participant Reactions," *Journal of Business and Psychology*: develops and validates a detection tool.
- Roebke, Bowling & Burns (2025), "The Effects of Careless Responding Warnings on the Construct Validity of Self-Report Measures," *Journal of Business and Psychology*: tests an intervention.
- Bowling, Sessa, Shaffer & Banks (2026), "Read my lips: No new constructs! Construct proliferation as a threat to the future of I-O psychology," *Industrial and Organizational Psychology*: critique of construct redundancy and jangle.

**Would referee well:** careless/insufficient-effort responding, attention
checks and survey data screening, self-report measurement validity, construct
redundancy and discriminant validity (jingle-jangle), meta-analytic reviews,
scale validation for job-attitude measures.
